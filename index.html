MERC DROP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

  • MERC DROP — com.mercdrop.game
  • MERC DROP DEMO — com.mercdrop.demo

Short version: the game collects nothing about you.

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else. Your settings and your progress are saved on your own device and stay there.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Android games MERC DROP and MERC DROP DEMO ("the game"), published on Google Play by the developer of MERC DROP ("we", "us"). It does not cover any other app, website or service.

Questions about this policy or the game: xcompanygames@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Specifically, it does not request, read or send:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account identifier — the game has no sign-in of any kind;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, files, calendar or call history;
  • your camera or microphone — the game does not request those permissions at all;
  • advertising identifiers, device identifiers or any other tracking ID.

We do not use any advertising network, analytics service, crash-reporting service, attribution service or social SDK. The game is built on the open-source LÖVE / SDL game engine, which does not collect data either.

3. What the game saves on your device

So that the game can remember your progress and your preferences, it writes the following into its own private storage area on your device:

  • Settings — audio volumes, display and control options.
  • Control bindings — your keyboard, gamepad and touch layout choices.
  • Progress — campaign checkpoint, unlocked content and high score.
  • Diagnostic logs — a plain-text session log and, if the game ever crashes, a crash file. These contain only technical information: the game version, the operating-system name reported by the device (for example "Android"), how many minutes the session lasted, the stage, score and kill count reached, and the error message with its technical traceback if a crash occurred. They contain no personal or device-identifying information.

All of this stays on your device. These files are never uploaded to us. Other apps cannot read them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Android backup

If you have Android's built-in backup switched on in your device settings, Android may copy the game's save folder into your personal Google account backup, so that your progress returns when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it and receive nothing from it. You can turn Android backup off in your device settings at any time.

4. Permissions the game requests, and why

PermissionWhy it is there
VIBRATE Rumble feedback on your phone and on connected controllers.
BLUETOOTH Lets the game recognise a Bluetooth game controller and its motors. It is not used to scan for, identify or connect to any other device.
INTERNET Declared by the LÖVE game engine that the game is built on. The game itself makes no network requests: it does not download, upload or check anything online, and it works fully offline.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Legacy engine entry limited to Android 4.3 and older. It has no effect on any Android version the game supports (Android 6.0 and newer).

The game requests no other permissions. It never asks for location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

5. Links that leave the game

The title screen offers optional ways to reach us. You only ever leave the game if you choose to tap or scan them:

  • a Discord button, which opens our community invite in your browser or Discord app;
  • on-screen QR codes for rating the game on Google Play and for an optional feedback form.

Those destinations are run by third parties (Discord, Google) under their own privacy policies, and anything you choose to type into the feedback form is sent to us through that form — never automatically by the game. Nothing is sent unless you deliberately do it.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It contains stylised action violence and is rated accordingly on Google Play. Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

7. Data retention and your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain, correct, export or delete, and no request is needed. Everything the game stores lives on your device and is under your control:

  • uninstalling the game deletes its saves, settings and logs;
  • Android Settings → Apps → MERC DROP → Storage → "Clear data" resets it without uninstalling.

If you still have a question about your privacy, write to xcompanygames@gmail.com and we will answer.
