Short version

The game has no ads and no advertising or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data. It sends a small amount of data to run its online features: the leaderboards (the player name you choose and your scores), anonymous play stats that help us balance the game (you can turn these off), any feedback you choose to write to us, and Google Play services on Android.

We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or GPS location.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the games MERC DROP, MERC DROP EARLY ACCESS and MERC DROP DEMO ("the game") on Android and Windows, made by the developer of MERC DROP ("we", "us"). It does not cover any other app, website or service.

Questions about this policy or the game: xcompanygames@gmail.com.

2. What the game sends, and why

2.1 Online leaderboards (SURVIVAL and CLASSIC)

When you beat your own best score in SURVIVAL or CLASSIC, the game sends that score to the online leaderboard. It sends:

  • a random anonymous account ID, created for your install by Firebase Authentication (Google). It is not linked to your Google account, email address or phone number;
  • the player name you typed for your profile (names pass a word filter);
  • the score and how it was made: kills, the level or wave reached and the run's play time, plus the time the score was sent.

Leaderboards are public: every player can see the top names and scores in the game, including on the title screen. We use this data only to run the leaderboards and to refuse impossible scores. There is no switch to stop sending scores. If you would rather not appear, use a player name that doesn't identify you, or write to us and we will remove your entry (see section 9).

2.2 Play stats (optional, on by default)

To see where the game is too hard or too easy, which bugs players hit and which devices and controllers to support, the game sends anonymous play statistics. They use a separate anonymous account ID for your install and carry no player name. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > GAMEPLAY > SHARE PLAY STATS. Turning the switch off stops recording and deletes any stats not yet sent.

The play stats contain:

  • How each run went: the mode, the stages, waves or levels reached and cleared, the time spent on each, deaths and what caused them, the guns used and how accurately, the enemies met, the pickups found, the score and kills;
  • A trace of each run (from version 0.0.236): where your soldier and the enemies moved on the game's map, hits, deaths and pickups. These are positions inside the game world, not real-world locations;
  • Sessions: how long the game was open and played, and how many times it has been launched on this install;
  • Your setup: the game version and edition, the operating system, the device class (phone, tablet or PC), the input type (touch, controller or keyboard) and the controller model (for example "DualSense"), the game's frame rate, and, when two people play together, how they were connected (one device, Wi-Fi, LAN or Bluetooth) and the connection quality;
  • Your region setting (from version 0.0.236): the two-letter country code of the region chosen in your device's language and region settings (for example "US"). It is not taken from GPS, from your network or from your IP address, and the game needs no permission to read it.

We only use play stats in totals and to study single runs without knowing who played them. The game also shows some of these totals to all players, with no names: for example, how many players have reached the furthest stage, or what share of players reach each stage.

2.3 Feedback you send

The title screen's FEEDBACK button lets you write to us. When you press SEND, the game sends the text you wrote (up to 500 characters) along with the game version, the platform, the device class and the input type. It is delivered to our studio inbox by email through Formspree and a copy is stored in Firebase under your install's anonymous play stats ID. Nothing is sent unless you write it and press SEND.

2.4 Google Play services (Android, Google Play version)

  • Licence check: the game asks Google Play (Play Integrity, through Firebase App Check) to confirm that the copy was installed from Google Play. Google processes this check under its own terms. We receive only a pass or fail token.
  • Google Play Games (optional): if you sign in, Google Play Games stores your achievements and, if CLOUD SAVE is on, a copy of your saved progress in your Google account. You can turn both off in Settings > GAMEPLAY. The game shows your Play Games name in Settings; it stays on your device. We do not receive your Google account details.
  • Updates: Google Play tells the game when a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update.

2.5 Technical data every connection carries

Like any internet connection, the game's requests reach Google's servers (Firebase) from your IP address. Google uses it to deliver the data and to protect the service from abuse. We do not store IP addresses with your records and do not use them to find your location.

3. What the game does not collect

  • your real name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • your precise or GPS location;
  • your contacts, photos, files, messages, calendar or call history;
  • your camera or microphone;
  • advertising IDs. The game has no ads and no advertising, attribution or third-party analytics SDKs.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Who handles the data for us

  • Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase App Check store and protect the leaderboards, play stats and feedback copies; Google Play services run the licence check, Play Games and updates. Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy.
  • Formspree delivers feedback messages to our inbox by email. Formspree Privacy Policy.

These providers process the data on our behalf. We do not pass it to anyone else.

5. Playing together (MULTIPLAYER)

When two devices play together, they connect to each other directly over Bluetooth or over your local Wi-Fi or LAN. They exchange each player's profile name, outfit, game version and the live state of the game. This traffic stays between the two devices on your local connection and does not pass through our servers. To find each other on Wi-Fi, the devices send a short announcement on the local network.

6. What the game saves on your device

The game keeps your settings, control bindings, touch layout, profiles (player names, gold, outfits and skins, achievements), campaign progress and a local diagnostic log in its own private storage. The anonymous account IDs from section 2 and any play stats or feedback not yet sent wait there too. The diagnostic log is not uploaded. Uninstalling the game, or Android Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → "Clear data", deletes all of it.

Android backup

If Android's built-in backup is on, Android may copy the game's save folder into your Google account backup so your progress returns on a new phone. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

8. Security

Every request is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The database only lets your install write its own records. Play stats and feedback can be read by the developer only, while leaderboard names and scores are public by design.

9. How long we keep data, and your choices

  • Leaderboards: entries stay while the leaderboards run. To have yours removed, email us your player name and the mode (SURVIVAL or CLASSIC).
  • Play stats and feedback: kept while they help us improve the game, then deleted. Play stats carry no name, so we cannot tell which records are yours. Turn off SHARE PLAY STATS to stop them; uninstalling or clearing the game's data gives your next install a new anonymous ID.
  • Google Play Games: sign out or turn it off in Settings > GAMEPLAY; your Play Games data is managed in your Google account.

For any other question about your data, or to ask for a copy or for deletion, write to xcompanygames@gmail.com and we will answer.

10. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It contains stylised action violence and is rated accordingly on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
